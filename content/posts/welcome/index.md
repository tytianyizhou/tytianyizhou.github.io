Hi, and welcome! :wave:

I'm starting this section as a place to **gradually share my learning notes, thoughts, and insights**
from research and everyday reading. Writing things down helps me understand them more deeply, and I
hope some of these notes will be useful to others too.

## What to expect

- **Learning notes:** structured write-ups on topics I'm studying, with derivations and code where helpful.
- **Paper reading:** summaries and reflections on papers I find interesting, and how they connect to each other.
- **Thoughts and insights:** shorter pieces on open questions, research directions, and lessons learned along the way.

Topics will mostly be around **reliable and safe language models** (uncertainty, hallucination,
evaluation, post-training, and alignment), **optimization for LLM training**, and
**responsible algorithms for online information systems**.

## How it works

Notes will be updated gradually; some may start rough and get refined over time. You can browse them
by [tags](/tags/) or the [archive](/archives/), use [search](/search/), or follow along via
[RSS](/index.xml).

If you have comments, corrections, or just want to chat about any of these topics, feel free to
[reach out](mailto:tyzhou@stanford.edu).

Stay tuned! :rocket:
