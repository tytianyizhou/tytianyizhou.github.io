Hi! I'm **Tianyi Zhou**, a PhD student in Computer Science at
[KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/profile/tzho), advised by
[Aristides Gionis](https://www.kth.se/profile/argioni?l=en), and currently a visiting PhD student at
**Stanford University**. I am also an affiliated doctoral researcher in the
[WASP](https://wasp-sweden.org/) program (AI/ML track).

My research aims to build **safe, capable, and responsible AI systems**. I focus on the
evaluation, post-training, and alignment of frontier models, and on how to identify and mitigate
the risks that emerge as language models become more capable and agentic. In particular, I study how
**uncertainty-aware generation and verification** can support safe and controllable model behavior
while preserving reasoning and problem-solving capabilities.

Earlier, I studied how algorithmic design in online information systems (social networks, news
platforms, and recommender systems) can amplify harms such as polarization and viewpoint imbalance,
and how these harms can be mitigated.
